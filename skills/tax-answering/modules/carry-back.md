# Carry-back relief: unabsorbed capital allowances and trade losses

## Where to look

- Relief and claimants: s 37D(1); paras 2.1, 3.2, 3.4, 5.1-5.2.
- Qualifying deductions (QD): s 37D(9); paras 3.5, 3.9-3.10. Exclusions: s 37D(10), (16A), (16B), (18); para 8.1.
- Amount limits: s 37D(3), (5), (17); paras 2.3, 3.1, 6.5-6.7; Annex A.
- Order of deduction: s 37D(2); paras 6.8-6.10; Annexes B, F.
- Same business test: s 37D(11); paras 3.7, 6.2.
- Shareholding test and waiver: s 37D(12)-(16); paras 3.8, 6.3-6.4.
- Concessionary-rate income: s 37D(4)-(5), (17); paras 7.1-7.3; Annexes C, D1, D2.
- Group relief: paras 2.5, 7.4; Annex E.
- Individuals: paras 7.6-7.7. Limited partners: paras 7.8-7.10; Annex H.
- Multi-year carry-back: s 37D(1A)-(1D), (3A), (8A); para 2.2.
- Election, refund, reassessment: s 37D(6)-(8); paras 9.1-11.2.

## Check before answering

- Is each item a QD and not excluded?
- Which tests cover each capital allowance and trade loss (para 6.2)?
- If the test is waived, read s 37D(16) and all of para 6.4. Name the restriction.
- Is the rule keyed to the YA the QD arise in or the receiving YA? Read the exact words (such as paras 6.7, 7.9).
- Where does any unused balance go, and on what conditions (para 6.6)? Add no fallback from other guides.
- Does the multi-year rule cover this YA? If not, give only the basic rule.

## How to work it out

1. Amount: compare the QD left after any group relief transfer (para 7.4, s 37D(9)), the receiving YA's assessable income as s 37D(17) defines it, and the cap. Take the lowest.
2. Order: item order in s 37D(2), then income-source order and proration in paras 6.9-6.10.
3. Shareholding test: one row per item: its two dates from para 3.8, converted as para 6.3 does. Put them in calendar order; the source's first date can be the later one. Compare who held the shares on each date. It fails only if shares held by the same persons on both dates fall below what s 37D(13) requires. Shares held through a company count as its shareholders' (s 37D(13)(b)).
4. Waiver: give its condition, then its restriction.
5. Concessionary rate: take the operations from para 7.2; the Act text loses its signs. Which is asked: the amount counted toward the cap (para 7.2), or the amount deductible against concessionary-rate income (Annexes C-D2)? Copy that source's operation; never reverse it. Test it with a sample figure. Formula, working and conclusion must agree.
6. Limited partner: check in para 7.9 which YA's capital applies.
7. Reply: keep tables in your reasoning. Conclusion first, then one line per item with its [n]. Always give a final answer.
