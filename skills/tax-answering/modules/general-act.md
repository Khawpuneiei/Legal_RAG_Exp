# General Income Tax Act provisions

## Where to look

- Definitions: s 2(1). Company residence: use the company limb. A section's "In this section" subsection defines terms for that section only (e.g. s 43(10)).
- Charge and source: s 10, s 12; appropriated trading stock s 10J.
- Exemptions: s 13(1) paragraphs; foreign-sourced income s 13(8) to (10).
- Rates: s 43(1); non-resident gross rates s 43(3), (3A), (7); partial and start-up exemptions s 43(6) to (6D), (10) to (12).
- Withholding s 45, 45A; anti-avoidance s 33, 34D; payment s 85, 87; offences s 94A, 95, 96, 96A; treaty credits s 49, 50.

## Check before answering

- Former section number in the question? Match the question's subject to a source heading; the old number may now belong to another provision. Never refuse over a missing old number.
- Does the source govern the person asked about (the company, its investors or employees)? Scheme nicknames may not appear; scan the sources for the operative term. None covers it? Use step 8; never say the Act lacks it.
- Definition: what does it include, exclude and deem?
- "Subject to" or "despite" another subsection? Apply the one that prevails.
- A later "does not apply", "excluding" or "to avoid doubt" subsection? Read it.
- Date or year bands? Pick the band the facts select; with no date, the latest stated band. Never list superseded bands.
- Conditions joined by "and" or "or"? Test each. Which condition does a waiver cover?
- Penalty tier: plain error, negligence, or wilful or fraudulent conduct? Any ceiling or remission power?
- Same citation in several sources? Read every part.
- Bracketed amendment notes are not conditions; deleted paragraphs are not law.
- Source points to a provision you lack? Answer the rest; name the gap.

## How to work it out

1. Write the one-sentence answer first.
2. List conditions, then exceptions, then any overriding subsection.
3. Deeming rule: state the trigger event, its date, the amount's measure and the period it falls in.
4. Rate: confirm income type, payer and recipient tests and date band; apply the rate to the base named (gross or chargeable).
5. Tiered exemption: is each slice's fraction chargeable or exempt? Apply slices in order, then the rate; test a sample figure.
6. Penalty: apply the stated multiple or rate to the stated base; add any further penalty once triggered; apply any stated cap only to what it limits.
7. Use only the question's figures; otherwise describe the method.
8. Cite [n] for every statement. If no source answers any part, give only the exact refusal sentence; otherwise answer the covered part, naming the gap.
