# Renovation Deductions, Other Deductions and Capital Allowances

## Where to look

- Renovation or refurbishment (R&R) deduction: s14N. R&R guide paras 4.1-4.3 (spread, option, cessation), 5.1-5.5 (cap period), 6.1 (double deduction), 7.1-7.2 (unused amounts), 8.1-8.4 (what qualifies), 9.1 (election), para 12 Annex (claim timing, pre-commencement, replacements, mixed use, missing approval, s10D businesses).
- Repairs: s14(1)(c). Disallowed items: s15.
- Vacant rental property: s14ZF. Overseas-posting salary: s14I, definitions in s14I(18).
- Plant and machinery: s19, s19A. Balancing adjustments: s20, s21. Set-off order: s22A. Carry forward: s23. Controlled sales: s24. Conversions, such as to an LLP: s25.

## Check before answering

- Repair with no improvement, or capital R&R? Can both reliefs apply to the same spend (para 6.1, s14N(7)(a))?
- Do the works need Building Control approval? Is the item excluded, and which YA rule governs designer fees (s14N(9), para 8.3)?
- Which cap period covers the YA: moving, fixed, or transitional (paras 5.3-5.5, s14N(8))?
- Claimed in the YA the spend was incurred? Pre-commencement spend? Trade ceased (s14N(2), (4), (7)(b))?
- Unused R&R deduction: list every route and condition in para 7.2. Reread all of it before calling it silent.
- Wrong claim found later: which YA bears the deemed income, the YA claimed or the YA of discovery (s14N(5))?
- Balancing charge: what cap applies (s20(4))? Is open-market price substituted (s20(1A), (2))?
- Allowances carried forward: do s23(4) or (6) restrict them?
- Transfer election: written notice? Income-producing use on both sides? s33 excluded (s24(1), (3), (4); s25(1), (3), (4))?
- Question term not in the sources? Use the source's term for that quantity, say so, and do not suggest they differ.
- Defined terms: what is included and excluded? Comptroller-satisfaction tests, such as s14ZF(3): state the deduction as conditional.
- Former section number? Sources show current numbers; use the source on that subject.

## How to work it out

R&R deduction:
1. Confirm the trade continues and each item qualifies.
2. Total qualifying spend in the cap period. Disallow the excess (s14N(7)(f)).
3. Spread or full deduction? Check which one needs an election (s14N(3), (3A), (3B)).
4. Deduct after other Part 5 deductions (para 7.1). Route any unused amount by para 7.2.

Claw-back: name the provision and YA of charge. Use only the question's figures; if missing, describe the method.

Balancing adjustment:
1. Take moneys received, or the substituted open-market price.
2. Compare with expenditure still unallowed.
3. Moneys lower or none: balancing allowance (s20(2A)). Higher: charge (s20(3)).
4. Cap the charge (s20(4)). Check for a s21 election.
5. Run the question's numbers through each step.

Set-off: apply s22A in order, then s23.

Answer conclusion first, cite every statement with [n], stay under 200 words.
