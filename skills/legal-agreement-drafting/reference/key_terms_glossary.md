- **Losses** (`DEF-LOSSES`): all losses, liabilities, damages, costs (including reasonable legal fees) and expenses, but excluding indirect or consequential loss and loss of profit.
- **Management Fee** (`DEF-MANAGEMENT-FEE`): the fee payable to the Investment Manager for its management services, calculated in accordance with Clause 9 (Management Fee).
- **Net Asset Value** (`DEF-NAV`): the value of the assets of the Portfolio or the Fund (as the context requires) less its liabilities, calculated by the administrator in accordance with the valuation policy in force.
- **Performance Fee** (`DEF-PERFORMANCE-FEE`): the fee payable to the Investment Manager by reference to the increase in Net Asset Value above the High-Water Mark.
