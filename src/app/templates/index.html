AI-assisted agreement drafting, with Legal in control

Staff submit a structured request. The system picks the approved template, fills the fields, matches each special request to an approved clause, checks the request against the drafting policy and routes the draft to the right reviewer. Nothing is executed without human approval, and every step is written to a tamper-evident audit trail.

Request form→PII guard→Template router→Field populator→Clause retrieval + A* assembly→Validation engine→Routing policy→Legal / Senior Legal review→Audit trail
