AI governance

How the system is designed to meet the MAS Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management (issued 7 October 2026; sections 3–4 apply from 7 October 2027, sections 5–6 by 7 October 2028). The full mapping and the AI inventory entry are in docs/governance.md.

AI inventory entry

Use case
Drafting support for standard agreements (NDA, IMA, distribution, side letter, subscription)
Decision role
Assists Legal; never executes. Every draft needs human approval; escalated drafts need Senior Legal / Compliance.
Model(s)
Deployment mode {{ mode }}. Rules mode uses no generative model. Assist/agent modes use an approved LLM behind an OpenAI-compatible gateway (evaluated: qwen2.5:7b local).
Data
Synthetic requests and a synthetic clause library. PII masked before storage or any LLM call.
Proposed materiality
Lower (internal drafting copilot with mandatory human review, footnotes 12–13), subject to the control function's approval (4.13).
Versions
policy {{ policy_sha }}, library {{ library_sha }}; both are written into every audit event.

Controls

Human oversight (5.9)Routing policy with pre-defined escalation conditions; approval only via api_review; decisions and near misses logged.
Auditability (5.17)Hash-chained, append-only audit events with policy, library, code and prompt hashes.
Testing (5.14, fn 46)Scoreboard on a frozen test set; prompt-injection and hallucination metrics; release gate in CI.
Third-party AI (5.10)Model behind a gateway; replaceable; tested on our own data; compensating controls are the rules engine and human review.
Data (2.5(b))No confidential data to public tools: PII guard, synthetic data in the demo, region-pinned services (Singapore).
